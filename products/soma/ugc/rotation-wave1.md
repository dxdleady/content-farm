## Program-naming policy (pillar B auto-fill decks)

Per the 2026-08-31 competitive/legal research: workout routines are not
copyrightable (Bikram v. Evolation, 9th Cir. 2015) but program NAMES are
trademarks with real enforcement history (Wendler C&D 2012; Active
Network v. RunSignUp over "Couch to 5K"). Until the in-app curated-library
legal review clears:

- copy says "the 5x5 everyone runs", "the 9-week running plan" — never
  StrongLifts / Couch to 5K / C25K / 5/3/1 / Air Alert;
- no frame may show the app's program-chips row (the shot list already
  skips it);
- revisit and possibly re-cut with names after legal review.
